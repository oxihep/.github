# Code of conduct

The oxihep repositories follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and
discussions; criticize ideas and code, not people; no harassment, insults or
personal attacks, in public or in private.

To report behavior that breaks it, contact the maintainer privately through
the contact details on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/MathieuOuillon),
or use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block someone
from the organization, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
